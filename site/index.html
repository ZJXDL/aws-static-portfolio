HOW TO PLAY

A little logic
goes a long way.

1

Open a square. The first one is always safe.

2

Follow the numbers. Each number counts nearby mines. Click it when matching flags are in place to clear its other neighbors.

3

Flag what you know. Right-click, tap Flag mode, or press F.

4

Clear every safe square. Use arrow keys to move, then Enter to open.
